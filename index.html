Model fit indices & comparative statistics

Lower AIC/BIC/SABIC indicate better fit (best BIC among K ≥ 2 is highlighted). BLRT p and LMR p both test K profiles against K-1 (p < .05 favors K). LMR p uses the adjusted Lo-Mendell-Rubin formula as implemented in tidyLPA's calc_lrt; it is not guaranteed to equal Mplus's VLMR. "Best-LL starts" = how many random starts reached the best LogLik (within 0.001): a value of 1 means the optimum was found only once, so increase the number of starts. Entropy and AvePP > .80 indicate good classification.

BIC across covariance structures

Lower BIC is better. A "—" means no solution converged for that cell. Comparing EEI with EEE/VVV shows how strongly the zero-covariance (local independence) assumption is rejected.
